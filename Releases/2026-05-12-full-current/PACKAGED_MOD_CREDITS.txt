Kuray Infinite Fusion Packaged Mod Credits

This tester build bundles the current local mod stack plus the current KIF multiplayer files.
Author labels below come from each included mod's own metadata when available.

Included multiplayer
- KIF multiplayer client scripts under Data\\Scripts\\659_Multiplayer and the KIFM companion server/runtime files are included in this build.

Included mods
- Autoplay Dex Bot (autoplay_bot, v0.1.0) | Author: OpenAI Codex | Adds a pausable in-game autoplay bot that uses virtual inputs, scans maps/events, follows guide objectives, and tracks dex progress. This first release is intentionally conservative: it can plan, explore, battle with existing AI, catch needed species, and pause for manual takeover when uncertain.
- Counterfeit Shinies (counterfeit_shinies, v1.0.0) | Author: Probably | Adds Team Infinite Cannon's counterfeit shiny racket: forged palettes, buyer NPC APIs, enforcer pressure, prestige, and black-market sales.
- Custom Species Framework (custom_species_framework, v1.1.0) | Author: OpenAI Codex | Production-quality framework for custom Fakemon, regional variants, browser-based species creation, starter integration, fusion rules, and future content hooks.
- Diagonal Movement (diagonal_movement, v1.0.0) | Author: Probably | Enables 8-direction movement and a diagonal movement option.
- Mouse UI (mouse_ui, v1.0.0) | Author: Probably | Adds mouse-driven UI interaction, battle button clicks, and edge-walk map controls.
- PC Shopping (pc_shopping, v1.0.0) | Author: Unknown | Adds Infinitely Instant Delivery PC shopping and battle betting features.
- PC Storage Sorter (pc_storage_sorter, v1.0.0) | Author: Codex | Adds focused PC storage sorting options for the current box or all boxes, leaving Box 1 open when sorting the full collection.
- Player Identity and Bedroom (player_identity_bedroom, v0.1.0) | Author: Probably | Adds a nonbinary player option with they/them pronouns and decouples bedroom style from gender. Uses intro/menu aliases plus targeted event/common-event overrides to stay compatible with the Kuray framework.
- Pokegun Terminal (pokegun_terminal, v1.0.0) | Author: Probably | Adds a Pokegun PC terminal that sells specialty guns and ammo.
- Safe Save Fallbacks (safe_save_fallbacks, v0.1.0) | Author: KIF | Adds compatibility shims and safe relocation for saves that still reference removable framework or map-content mods. Keep this mod installed when testing uninstall/reinstall flows for custom species, travel expansions, player identity bedroom, or other removable map content.
- Travel Expansion Framework (travel_expansion_framework, v0.2.0-rc.1) | Author: OpenAI Codex | Mounts expansion-pack regions as managed mods instead of hard-merging them into the host game. Provides manifest scanning, namespaced registries, expansion save state, virtual map IDs, bedroom-PC travel, trainer-card progress pages, and modder tooling.
- Uncap Trainer Rematch Level (uncap_trainer_rematch_level, v1.0.0) | Author: An Unsocial Pigeon | Removes the level cap applied to trainer rematch scaling. Converted to the folder-based KIF mod loader format.
